HONGDAE · CHARCOAL GRILLED EEL

Premium Korean Eel, Perfected Over Oak Charcoal

A Hongdae grilled-eel house where live eels are prepared in-house and grilled over charcoal.

01 · PREMIUM EEL

Only the Finest Eel

We use only domestic Japonica eel from suppliers certified by Suhyup, Korea's National Federation of Fisheries Cooperatives. Raised healthy in clean waters, every eel goes through strict hygiene control and expert preparation to reach you at peak freshness.

02 · CHARCOAL & SAUCE

Fine Charcoal, Signature Sauce, Roasted Salt

Premium oak charcoal, fired twice for extra density, brings out the natural flavor of the eel. Our house-developed signature sauce and roasted salt deepen its rich, savory taste even further.

03 · LIVE EEL EXPERIENCE

A Live Eel Experience

Touch a live eel and snap a photo before it hits the grill. Prepared on the spot and grilled straight over charcoal, the eel sheds just the right amount of fat while staying tender and juicy.
